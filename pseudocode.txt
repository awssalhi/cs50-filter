Blur pseudocode

1. Make a copy of the whole image before changing anything.
2. Go through every pixel in the image (row i, column j).
3.   Start the red sum, green sum, blue sum and the count at 0.
4.   Look at the 3x3 box around the pixel: rows i-1 to i+1, columns j-1 to j+1.
5.   For each spot in the box, check whether it exists on the image:
     its row must be between 0 and height-1, and its column between 0 and width-1.
6.   If the spot exists, add its red, green and blue FROM THE COPY to the sums, and add 1 to the count.
7.   At a corner, 5 of the 9 spots fall off the image, so only 4 are counted.
     (Top-left corner: row -1 and column -1 do not exist.) On an edge 6 are counted, in the middle 9.
8.   Set the real pixel to each sum divided by the count, as a decimal, rounded.
